Three Amigos

Turn-based card game where players take turns playing cards from their hand and table until a player has no more cards to play. The game is designed for 2-4 players and combines strategy with luck.

How to Play

Each player starts with a total of 9 cards: a hand of 3 cards and table of 6 cards placed in a 1x3 grid

The top 3 cards of the grid are visible to all players and can only be played once a player has no more cards in their hand.

The bottom 3 cards of the grid are hidden to all players and can only be played after the 3 top cards. This means that players can only see the top cards of their table and must play them before they can access the next cards below.

Players take turns playing cards from their hand to the main pile in the center of the table. After their turn, they draw a card from the deck to replenish their hand.

Cards must be played in ascending order, meaning that the card played must be of equal or higher value than the card on top of the pile. If a player cannot play a card, he must draw the pile and end his turn.

If a player has cards of equal value he can play them together in a single turn. If all 4 cards played in a turn are of the same value, the pile is cleared and removed from the game, and the player can play another card immediately.

If a player's turn results in the pile having 4 cards of the same value, the pile is cleared and removed from the game, and the player can play another card immediately.

Special Card Powers

Some cards have special powers that can affect the game:

1 - Can be played on any card except 7 and forces a chosen player to play.
2 - Can be played on any card and resets the pile to 0.
7 - Forces the next player to play a card of value 6 or lower.
8 - Forces the next player to skip their turn.
10 - Clears the pile, removing all cards from the game. Player can play another card immediately.

Objective

Players must strategically manage their hand and table cards to outplay their opponents. The first player to empty their hand and table wins the game.
